  Potosi, Bolivia Monday, May 2, 2016

Not a lot to say about this. Another day another bus..... Just beautiful scenery again and as always - most are taken from the bus window so forgive any shaky/wobbly ones. We are on our way to Potosi which is supposed to be a nice little town but with a great big silver mine which we are hoping to visit. Apparently you can go down it so that will be a first for us - going down a working mine.
