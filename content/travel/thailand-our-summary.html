  Satun, Thailand Thursday, December 4, 2014

Quick summary Currency ~ Thai Baht £1 = 50 baht Population ~ 67 million Capital City ~ Bangkok Our Budget ~ rooms: about £15 a night, not counting the nice room we need occasionally for a shower and a good nights sleep    ~ food: £5 each per day is plenty, They drive on the left - same as us Places we stayed and our Star Rating Shanghai Mansion - Bangkok - 10* Wild Orchid - Bangkok - 4* New Siam Guesthouse - 2* Best Guesthouse - Kho Samet SK1 - Chiang Mai - 2* SK2 - Chiang Mai - 3* 4T Guesthouse - Sukhothai - 4* Chumphong Gardens Hotel - Chumphong 3* Hin Wong Apartments - Hin Wong Bay, Koh Tao 4* Blue Wind Resort - Sairee Beach, Koh Tao 3* Jam Thong Guesthouse - Mae Haad, Koh Tao 3* Jinta City Hotel - Na Tong Koh Samui 1* Viva Vacation - Na Tong, Koh Samui 10****** Cliffs Hostel & Art Cafe - Satun 2* Summary What was different this time ~ Again we didn't have the wow factor of being here for the first time so it felt a bit routine
a local
 
. We could get a full 30 day visa on arrival overland this time too which meant we had less time constraints without doing a "visa run" to the border. As we are moving more slowly on this trip, we didn't rush through Thailand to get somewhere else either which was great. We loved ~ the floating markets, The Bridge on the Kwai, WANALEEEEEE!! The north of Thailand, The Karen people, The White Temple, Koh Tao, Thai massage We didn't like ~ Satun (ferry town), Thailand is supposed to be the land of smiles, but in Bangkok it felt a bit more like we will smile when you show us your money The people ~ generally really lovely but in the big city there are many that only want your money and if something you have paid for isn't right and you want a refund, well, then you see a different side to them. The north of Thailand was very different from the south and the people were really lovely here. The islanders too are generally more laid back than Bangkok. Food ~ good for vegetarians. A lot of fresh fish eaten here too even inland because of the huge rivers Transport - Good transport links and it is really easy to get anywhere you want to go. Just say where and someone will sell you a ticket! Things which will remind us of Thailand ~ Elephants, sky lanterns Would we recommend it?  Yes definitely and will come back again
