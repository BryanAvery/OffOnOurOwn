My journey began not with a single trip, but with a feeling — that quiet restlessness we all get when life starts to feel too predictable. I wanted to see more, to feel more, to step beyond the familiar and experience the world with open eyes and an open heart. What began as a few spontaneous adventures soon became a way of life — exploring new places, meeting new people, and learning what it truly means to be free.

Over time, each journey has shaped me in ways I never expected. Travel has taught me patience when plans fall apart, humility when language fails, and gratitude in the smallest of things — a sunrise over the sea, a shared meal, a laugh with someone whose name I never learned. The world has a way of reminding us how connected we really are, and how much beauty exists when we simply slow down and look for it.

Through Off On Our Own, I’ve found a way to capture these experiences — the highs, the humour, the challenges, and the quiet reflections that come after. It’s not just about where I’ve been, but what I’ve discovered along the way: that life isn’t something to be rushed through or planned perfectly. It’s something to be lived — fully, messily, and with a sense of wonder.

This is my journey. A story still being written, one adventure at a time.
