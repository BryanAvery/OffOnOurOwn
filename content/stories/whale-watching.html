--- title: Whale Watching date: '2011-02-04T16:58:23Z' lastmod: '2011-02-04T16:58:23Z' url: /whale-watching/ legacy_id: '458' original_url: https://offonourown.co.uk/whale-watching/ legacy_type: post categories: - Uncategorized migration_notes: - Missing featured image metadata --- We spent a morning on a boat, 18 miles off shore, to watch four Sperm Whales swimming and showing off for us. They all where magnificent, and being fully grown males, they liked to show off their huge tails. Considering how big they are, they were so graceful and never even made a splash when they dived. amazing! This is one morning we will both remember for the rest of our lives.
