Highlights and Summary of the Skiing Trip to Avoriaz (20th–29th December 2024)

Key Highlights:

  1. Journey to Avoriaz:
    • Left on 20th December, travelling via Dover to Calais on the Irish Ferry.
    • Overnight stop in Le Vaudioux at a quaint, old-fashioned French hotel.
  2. Skiing Adventures:
    • Perfect snow conditions with plenty of powder and sunshine later in the week.
    • Dana and Bryan took ski lessons to learn carving, venturing into Switzerland and tackling challenging slopes.
    • Katja faced her fears and improved her skiing on blue and green runs.
    • Memorable stops at the Igloo Bar and La Folie Douce for drinks and après-ski fun.
  3. Family Moments:
    • Celebrated Adam and Katja’s 21st birthdays with Raclette, fondue, and an amusing (if slightly moody) dinner.
    • Played Uno in the bar, creating light-hearted fun during downtime.
  4. Memorable Experiences:
    • Skiing from France into Switzerland.
    • Bryan bravely trying escargot (snails) on the first night.
    • Dana is proving it’s never too late to learn skiing at 49.
    • Katja’s determination to master the slopes despite falls and challenges.
  5. Journey Home:
    • Stopped overnight in Dijon at the Holiday Inn for rest and a good meal.

Overall Summary:

The 2024 skiing trip to Avoriaz was an incredible experience filled with unforgettable moments, stunning Alpine scenery, and quality family time.
