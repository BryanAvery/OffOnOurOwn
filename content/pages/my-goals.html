My goals are simple, but they mean everything to me. I want to live a life filled with purpose, curiosity, and connection — one that leaves room for adventure, reflection, and growth. Through Off On Our Own, my aim is to share stories that inspire others to explore not only the world around them, but also the worlds within themselves.

I want to keep writing — honestly and openly — capturing the beauty, chaos, and humour of travel and life. I hope my words encourage others to take that first step toward something new, whether it’s a journey across the world or a quiet moment of change at home.

Another goal is to build a community of like-minded travellers, storytellers, and dreamers who believe in the power of experience over possessions, of curiosity over comfort, and of kindness wherever the road leads.

Ultimately, my goal is to keep discovering — new places, new people, and new perspectives — while staying true to the spirit that started it all: being off on our own, yet always connected to something much bigger.
