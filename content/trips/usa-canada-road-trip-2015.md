Starting in New York, this road trip crossed the United States through the Midwest and Yellowstone, then continued through the western states before heading north into Canada. The route included time in Manhattan, wide-open stretches between destinations and a final stop in Vancouver.

The entries from July through October 2015 gather the people, landscapes and detours along the way.
