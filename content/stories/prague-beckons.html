Ice Cream Světozor: A Sweet Treat in Prague

Ice Cream Světozor, situated in the heart of Prague, is a delightful spot for anyone with a sweet tooth. Known for its creamy, rich texture and high-quality ingredients, this parlour offers an array of flavours from classic vanilla to adventurous options like lavender or pistachio. The parlour’s modern, minimalist design creates a welcoming atmosphere, making it an ideal break while exploring the city. It’s a must-visit for anyone looking to enjoy a delicious treat amidst Prague’s vibrant streets.

After our ice cream indulgence, we took a leisurely walk around the Old Town Square with Katja and Adam. The highlight of our stroll was the renowned Astronomical Clock.

The Astronomical Clock: Prague’s Timeless Treasure

The Astronomical Clock, or Prague Orloj, is one of the city’s most iconic landmarks. Installed in the Old Town Hall in 1410, it’s the third-oldest astronomical clock in the world and the oldest one still operational. The clock’s intricate design includes a dial that displays the position of celestial bodies, a calendar with the signs of the zodiac, and a series of mechanical figures that perform at the top of each hour.

Every hour, the clock puts on a show: the figures of the Twelve Apostles appear in the windows above the clock face, while other moving figures, including Death (represented by a skeleton), ring a bell. This hourly spectacle draws crowds who gather to witness the mechanical figures in action and admire the clock’s historical craftsmanship. The clock’s beauty and complexity are a testament to medieval engineering and artistry, making it a must-see for visitors to Prague.

Kmotra: A Historic Pizza Experience

Kmotra is more than just a restaurant; it’s an experience steeped in Prague’s rich history. Located in the atmospheric cellars of an old building in Prague’s Old Town, the restaurant’s warm and inviting décor complements its centuries-old architecture. Known for its exceptional pizzas, Kmotra offers a menu that ranges from classic Margherita to inventive creations, all prepared with fresh, high-quality ingredients. The thin, crisp crust and generously layered toppings make each pizza a flavourful delight. Dining here is as much about the setting as it is about the food, with the historic vaults providing a unique backdrop that enhances the overall experience.
