Bryan was so glad that they’ed booked it together, the reviews on the Internet where the best hotel in its price range. To describe the room, well the movies of a room that is a dark, dirty, old, falling apart and you just can’t describe it in words, we would have taken a photo, but we just want to forget that room. We got them to change our room, up to the 18th floor, much nicer, but still dirty, but somewhere for us to sleep the night. Walking around Times Square was a moment in time that we’ll both remember as the start of our world tour, very busy, as you’d expect.
