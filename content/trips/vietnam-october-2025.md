We travelled from London to Vietnam for a holiday that took us from the busy streets of Hanoi to the islands and beaches of Phú Quốc, with a cruise through Ha Long Bay along the way.

Hanoi gave us history, busy crossings, delicious food and a rooftop stay near Hoàn Kiếm Lake. Ha Long Bay brought a completely different kind of day, surrounded by limestone islands and dramatic scenery. Then we flew south to Phú Quốc, where the pace slowed down for beach days, snorkelling, markets and boat trips.

The trip had its share of tropical rain, unexpected detours and funny moments. These stories collect the places we visited and the small discoveries that made our Vietnam holiday ours.
