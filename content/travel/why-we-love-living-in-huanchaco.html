Not too much to say about this really.  The pictures will speak for themselves. On one particular day as Jody was walking around, she decided to take some pictures of all the flowers she came across in the town. Not really expecting to see many as it is very dry and dusty here, she was really surprised at just how many there are. Here and there are small patches of green haven and well tended flower beds. Some of them are not as dust free as others but we have included some of the better pictures here. It seems as if there is a really secret green-fingered community here. Surprisingly we don't get to relax and enjoy the beach that often but we see it every day whether walking passed it to get to school or Spanish lessons, and of course Bryan gets to see it most days when he is taking the kids swimming. As it is west facing, we get the sun sets and sometimes the sky is too hazy to really appreciate it. But when it's clear - the sun sets are spectacular!
