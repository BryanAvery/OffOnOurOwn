The journey moved from northern Peru, with stops including Trujillo and Huaraz, to Mexico City. From there it continued to Playa del Carmen, where time by the coast included a visit to a cenote.

The route also reached Caracol in Belize, a Maya site deep in the forest, before returning to Playa del Carmen. These entries follow the trip from February to March 2017.
