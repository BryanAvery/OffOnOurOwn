However, you can’t judge a city by one nights accommodation, and our first impressions after our first evening walking around is a vibrant and happening city. Friday, 10 December 2010 Moved to HI Hostels and joined their membership, as we are sure we will be using them quite a lot during our travels. booked our trip to Niagara and up the CN tower with them so got good price. We are both very tired so crashed and slept a few hours this afternoon. Bryan woke up feeling like dancing so off he went on his own into the city. Jody stayed in and had a bit of a pamper - got to use that hairdryer!! Saturday, 11 December 2010 we went to watch Harry Potter today. i’m still a bit confused as to who’s the good guys and who are the baddies. but we enjoyed the film anyway went to a local brewery in the afternoon - Steam Whistle Brewery. had a few beers there before moving on to a bar where we had to pay for them! bought some groceries and a bottle of wine so we could eat in tonight and Bryan shaved off his beard!! hurray!! Sunday, 12 December 2010 we went for a walk in the rain, to the old part of Toronto today. we wondered if we were going the right way as it was all a bit tower blocky. when we got to the right place though, it was all quaint and christmassy. they had a christmas market on, a lovely big tree and a brass band. starting to feel a little bit of the christmas spirit now. oh no - that was the hot apple cider!
