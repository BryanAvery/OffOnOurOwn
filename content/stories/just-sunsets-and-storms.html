  Langkawi, Kedah, Malaysia Sunday, December 7, 2014

Nothing much to say other than all this beauty was right outside our door every night. Some nights there were just fabulous sunsets. Others there were incredible storms out at sea. We have seen some terrific thunderstorms in Thailand and now in Malaysia. The benefit here though is that being right on the beach, we can go there at night and watch it out at sea with uninterrupted views of it. Amazing! We did manage to get a few snaps of the lightning but never seemed to quite catch the forks of lightning as they hit the sea. Either way, we were never disappointed by the natural displays on offer. Who needs a tv with all this on the doorstep?
