It has its own territory and best of all it is free parking almost everywhere. We visited in Parliament House on Capital Hill, after a guided tour, seeing the Magna Carter we queued for the Prime Minister’s Question time in The House, which we were lucky to get in to. It was fun to watch, and we got up to speed with all the news in Japan and Christchurch. Interesting to watch how it all works and how relaxed all the MP’s were in the room. We spent so much time in Parliament House that we did not get any time to visit other parts of Canberra so we headed south to the coast Overall city experience : 3 - nothing there!
