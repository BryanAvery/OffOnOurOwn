It was really cold but lovely and sunny so we wrapped up warm and off we went! We wandered around the streets of New York, saw the Empire State Building, Grand Central Station, and Central Park, we giggled and laughed all the way around, even had a 99c 2-egg roll for breakfast, but not before having a $10 drink and cake in Starbucks first, note to self, don’t visit Starbucks otherwise we’ll blow our budget. Walking around New York is the only way to get around and see everything, the sights, the smells, and of course checking in at the Waldorf Hotel as we walked passed. yeah right - We wish. it would have been better than the ‘Grosstel’ Carter!!
