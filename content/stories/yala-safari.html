  Yala National Park, Sri Lanka Friday, July 11, 2014

Up at 4am and off in the jeep for our sunrise safari. We were were promised lots of animals and birds including peacocks, elephants and leopard. Our Driver Janaka got us there early so we were 3rd in the queue for tickets and as soon as he had them in his hand he raced back to the jeep and we were off! It was a beautiful morning and we did see many animals including Mongoose, Jackal, Crocodiles, Monkeys, Water Buffalo, Deer, Wild Pigs and the highlight of them all Elephants. And especially the baby elephant - he was so tiny.
Mother and baby
 
No Leopards for us i'm afraid but we were not that bothered. We saw a gazillion birds most of which we could not name and many we could not even photograph - they were too quick. Our highlights here were an Eagle, Peacocks and Peahens, Spoonbills, Cranes, Flamingoes, Pelicans and a brilliant Kingfisher.
