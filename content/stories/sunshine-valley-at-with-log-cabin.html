--- title: Sunshine Valley at with Log Cabin date: '2011-01-02T17:26:06Z' lastmod: '2011-01-02T17:26:06Z' url: /sunshine-valley-at-with-log-cabin/ legacy_id: '487' original_url: https://offonourown.co.uk/sunshine-valley-at-with-log-cabin/ legacy_type: post categories: - Uncategorized migration_notes: - Missing featured image metadata --- A two hour drive in to the Rockies, and we finally arrived at Sunshine Valley, now this is a fairy tail valley, tall pine trees and log cabins tucked away, it was so beautiful. After a few hot drinks and then we were off tobogganing, down the drive, it was so much fun. Afterwards we walked long to beautiful river, all frozen and crystal clear until we reached an old bridge about 3km up stream, the views in the valley were just breathtaking, snow covered trees and bright blue skies.
