--- title: Don Det - 4,000 islands date: '2011-05-11T15:53:50Z' lastmod: '2011-05-11T15:53:50Z' url: /don-det-4000-islands/ legacy_id: '331' original_url: https://offonourown.co.uk/don-det-4000-islands/ legacy_type: post categories: - Uncategorized migration_notes: - Missing featured image metadata --- The following day we left Pakse and caught a bus to the 4,000 Islands. Not knowing what to expect we were quite pleasantly surprised. The River Mekong is much cleaner here too. It didn’t take us long to find a nice room in the village for a good cheap price too. There are lots of bars and restaurants here which seem to engulf the small village it started from. A real backpacker destination with just enough lively spirit without being too over powering. For example, when you looked out of our window at the rear, you were staring at a muddy field full of water buffalo! There really wasn’t much to do here - we were treating it more as a place to chill and unwind. Apart from hiring bikes or a tuk tuk, or walking, there were no other ways to get around or indeed many places to go! Donlon Guest House Room rating: 7/10 - a palace compared to some on the island Location: 9/10 - perfect for the ferry Staff: 6/10 - all very young and a bit reserved Value for Money: 7/10 - £4.50 each a night Other comments: the worst bed we have slept on in a very long time Stay again? not unless they get new mattresses! Overall experience: 7/10 liked it here but we only stayed 2 nights. If it wasn’t for the dolphins the score would have been lower
