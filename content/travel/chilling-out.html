  Madiha, Southern, Sri Lanka Monday, July 7, 2014

Well we decided it was time to slow down a bit so we have come to a lovely quiet place to chill for a couple of days. Just outside the town of Matara is a tiny community called Madiha. We were actually heading for Marissa but we couldn't find any accommodation there so here we are. The big bonus of our £5 a night room is the fantastic view from our window and balcony; it is like we are hanging over the sea it is so close. The even BIG ger bonus is the turtle watching. The turtles come right up to shore to feed and every minute or so, they pop their heads up to breathe. They only appear for a second or so, so trying to get a photograph was pretty hard as you couldn't predict where they were going to pop up either.
