Dale drove us up to North Vancouver and to this beautiful canyon. there was a rope type bridge going over the gorge and the scenery was stunning. we walked down to the river at the bottom looking out for Bigfoot as we went. no signs of him or Mrs Bigfoot so we stayed a bit longer. then we headed off to Granville island which was beautiful too. we caught the little river ferry boat over and had a wander around. the best way i can describe it is like Covent Garden on a marina. a bottle of wine was in order before we headed back home. another great day
