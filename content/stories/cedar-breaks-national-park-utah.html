  Cedar Breaks National Monument, Utah Sunday, September 27, 2015

Well we did this National Park on the same day as Bryce Canyon and it was so spectacular (and unexpected) we have so many photos that we thought it deserved a mention all of it's own. We literally did just stumble upon this place. If the I9 hadn't been shut through Zion National Park then we wouldn't even have known about it. As it is, it's probably one of the most memorable because of the colours of the trees. Some of them were stunning - yep we use that word a lot but it is the best word for Cedar Breaks. Certainly at this time of the year anyway.
WOW WOW WOW
